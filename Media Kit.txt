DIGITAL CITIZENS NETWORK
MEDIA KIT


MISSION

The Digital Citizens Network exists to promote civic education, citizen journalism,
public research, legislative transparency, and community engagement through
decentralized media and volunteer participation.

WHO WE ARE
The Digital Citizens Network is a volunteer-driven media and information network
that provides educational resources, public policy research, citizen reporting,
podcast, newsletters, public discussions, and community events.

CORE OBJECTIVES
• Educate citizens on legislative and public policy issues
• Promote civil public discussion
• Support citizen journalism
• Encourage community participation
• Create publicly accessible research
• Foster transparency and accountability
